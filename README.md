# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Author:** Harith Parvez · PLP Data Analytics Cohort 7

## Business Problem
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall in Gikomba Market and recorded two weeks of daily sales and stock numbers manually. She needed an analytical breakdown to determine overall revenue growth, identify peak and sluggish business days, and discover which stock items require immediate reordering.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Overall Growth:** Total 14-day revenue reached KES 42,150, with Week 2 growing by 21.3% compared to Week 1.
2. **Weekend Dominance:** Weekend sales outperformed weekdays substantially, peaking at KES 5,600 on Saturday of Week 2.
3. **Critical Stock Deficits:** Both Tomatoes and Sukuma Wiki have fallen below their safety reorder thresholds and require urgent restocking.
